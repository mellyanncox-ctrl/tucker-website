Guide

What to do around the Tweed

Coffee first. Then pick one. All of it is a short drive from The Commons.

Walk Norries Headland
An 800-metre return boardwalk around the headland at Cabarita Beach. Then a swim.
Ride the Northern Rivers Rail Trail
The Tweed section runs 24km from Murwillumbah to Crabbes Creek, on sealed and compacted gravel paths with old rail bridges and tunnels along the way.
See the Margaret Olley Art Centre
At the Tweed Regional Gallery in Murwillumbah. Entry is free, and it's open 10 to 4, Wednesday to Sunday.

Where your dog can be off-leash

Four beaches on the Tweed coast, in council's own wording. Some maps online still have them the wrong way round.

South Kingscliff
From the Salt residential boundary north to the Cudgen Creek rockwall.
South Cabarita
200m south of Norries Headland to 1km north of Cudgera Creek.
South Fingal Head
From the Murphys Road access to 0.5km south of it.
South Pottsville
200m south of the main bathing area to 200m north of Black Rocks.
No dogs at all: Cudgera Creek, Hastings Point. The whole estuary and foreshore. It's home to the beach stone-curlew, one of roughly 16 left in NSW.

Dog beaches checked against tweed.nsw.gov.au, August 2026. Gallery hours from gallery.tweed.nsw.gov.au, October 2026.

Things people ask

What should I order?
The rosti benedict. Next question. Fine: two herbed potato rostis, poached eggs, house hollandaise and a topping of your choice. Gluten free, with a half serve for 15.5.
When does the kitchen close?
The kitchen runs 7:30am to 1pm every day. Coffee keeps going until 2pm.
Can I order ahead?
Yes. Order pickup online and it's waiting at the counter.
Is there a kids' menu?
Little Tucker is for under 12s: pancakes with maple, fried chicken bites with chips, avo or eggs on toast, from 8.
Do you do gluten free?
The rosti benedict is gluten free, and gluten-free bread is available on most dishes for 3.
Can I change a dish?
No. We keep the menu as written. If you want more, sides and add-ons are on the menu.
